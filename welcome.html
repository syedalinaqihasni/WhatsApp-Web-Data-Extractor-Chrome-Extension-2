How to use:

  1. Click the button above to open WhatsApp Web
  2. Log in to your WhatsApp account
  3. Click the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar
  4. Choose what you want to extract (chats or contacts)
  5. Select your preferred format and options
  6. Click Extract to download your data
